💡 What are ROL and ROI?

ROL (Net Operating Return) = gross income generated by the property before taxes and expenses.
ROI (Return on Investment) = net profit percentage relative to invested capital.
Fórmula: ROI = (Ingreso neto anual ÷ Inversión total) × 100
Example: a $150,000 property generating $18,000 net/year = ROI 12%.
